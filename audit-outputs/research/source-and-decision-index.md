## 1. Purpose

This index is the canonical cross-track source and decision map for the 25 Papyr primary research briefs. For every brief it records: the research question(s) it answers, its governing decisions, the approved-specification sections it serves, its authoritative sources with versions and access dates, its legacy and audit evidence paths, its recommendation summary, its dependencies and cross-track interfaces, and its material unresolved questions. It also records where sources are shared across briefs (deduplication), where claims or accepted decisions conflict (flagged, not resolved), and where source documentation is missing, stale, or unavailable.

Findings in every brief and in this index are recommendations, never accepted decisions (DEC-054, DEC-057). Nothing here authorizes implementation (DEC-060, DEC-188). This index feeds the Wave 4 reconciliation report (X2) and the owner-review handoff (plan §14).

## 2. Method

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, the research program plan, the full decision log (DEC-001 through DEC-188 plus the Open decisions section), the Product and UX Design Specification, and the Technical Architecture Specification.
2. Read every one of the 25 primary briefs in full at their exact paths under `audit-outputs/research/` (coverage table in Section 4).
3. Extracted per brief: research questions, governing decisions, spec sections, sources with versions and access dates, legacy evidence paths, recommendation summaries, interfaces, and unresolved questions.
4. Recorded shared sources (Section 6), flagged conflicts (Section 7), and missing or stale source documentation (Section 8).
5. Ran the completeness check in Section 9 against the plan's per-brief and program-wide assertions, restricted to what a read-only index can verify (no build, no git write, no execution).

Nothing in this index modifies any brief, decision, specification, audit, evidence file, or `papyr-reference/` content. All paths under `papyr-reference/` are read-only legacy reference (AGENTS.md; DEC-001, DEC-059).

## 3. Source precedence

The precedence applied throughout the briefs, and reflected in this index, is the plan's Section 5 and the architecture specification Section 1.4:

1. `papyr-rebuild-decisions.md`: authoritative record of accepted decisions.
2. `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-31-papyr-product-ux-design.md` and `2026-07-31-papyr-technical-architecture.md`: canonical design documents (DEC-185, DEC-188).
3. `audit-outputs/` files: durable evidence supporting design, not standalone requirements.
4. `papyr-reference/`: read-only legacy clone, evidence of the current baseline only (DEC-001, DEC-059).
5. Historical legacy documents in `papyr-reference/docs/`: non-canonical historical material (DEC-026).

External evidence within each brief ranks primary sources first per DEC-056: official documentation and source code above community material, license and advisory texts above summaries, current versions above stale ones. Every external source records a URL or identifier and an access date. All web sources in all 25 briefs were accessed 2026-07-31.
